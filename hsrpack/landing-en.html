FAQ

Can I get banned for this?

The app does not modify game files, does not read or change game memory and does not press keys for you. It only looks at the minimap image on screen (like screen recording) and draws its own window over the game. Still, it is an unofficial app and HoYoverse does not approve third-party software, so nobody can give a guarantee — use it at your own risk.

What are the requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The game must run in Windowed or Borderless mode — windows cannot be shown over exclusive fullscreen. No powerful PC needed: while your character stands still the app barely uses the CPU.

Do I need the internet?

Only to download maps and updates. If you download all maps in advance (Maps page), the app works offline. The key may be checked online once at activation.

My antivirus or Windows warns about the installer

The installer is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows SmartScreen warns about an “unknown publisher”. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. Download the app only from this page or from the GitHub releases page.

Can I use it on several PCs?

Yes, a key works on two of your PCs, and progress syncs between them (turn it on in Settings). If you changed your PC and the key does not activate, message the seller on FunPay and the binding will be reset.

Are updates paid?

No. While your key is valid, all new versions are free and install in one click right from the app.

Something doesn't work — who do I contact?

In the app: About → Report a problem — the report is saved to your desktop (it contains no key or passwords). Send it to the seller in FunPay chat: Zikzak283.
